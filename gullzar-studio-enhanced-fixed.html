Crafting Cinematic

Gullzar Studio is Lahore's premier wedding photography and videography studio, nestled in the heart of Al-Madina Colony Bazar Scheme. With over 8 years of crafting timeless memories, we blend artistry with technology to deliver breathtaking cinematic experiences.

From intimate Nikah ceremonies to grand Barat processions, our team of seasoned photographers and cinematographers capture every emotion, every smile, every tear — with unmatched precision and passion.

Policies

Terms & Conditions

Clear, honest policies — because your trust means everything to us.

📖
Album Policy
Each standard album includes 10 beautifully printed pages with lay-flat premium binding. Additional sheets are available at extra charge per page. Albums are crafted with archival-grade printing.
🔒
Privacy & Data Policy
As per our strict privacy policy, all personal photos and video data will be permanently deleted from our servers after 30 days of delivery. Please save all files promptly upon receiving your data.
🚫
Non-Refundable Advance
The 20% advance booking fee is strictly non-refundable, as it covers full team reservation, logistics planning, equipment preparation, and exclusive date blocking.
🎊
Post-Wedding Coverage
Additional charges apply for any coverage events after the main wedding ceremony — including post-wedding shoots, anniversary sessions, and extended walima events.
📱
Instagram Content Package
Dedicated Instagram Reels, Shorts, and social-media-ready content packages are available separately. Tag us @gullzar_studio to be featured on our official page!
📅
Booking Policy
Dates are confirmed only upon advance payment. We strongly recommend booking at least 2–3 months ahead, especially for peak wedding season (October–March).
